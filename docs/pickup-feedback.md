# Pickup delivery feedback

Coin/fuel collection uses the existing swept contact detection and original pickup sprites. Each newly collected item emits one event with its actual reward amount; fuel is capped at capacity. Physics rewards apply immediately, including fuel needed to continue riding.

A transparent Canvas 2D overlay, above HUD cards (z-index 10) and below dialogs (20), reuses the already loaded Pixi image resources. It launches the collected sprite from its current camera-projected position, follows one continuous cubic arc into the matching top-left HUD icon. The initial tangent is mostly upward and gradually bends left; one quintic easing curve gives zero velocity and acceleration at launch and docking, with no separate vertical/leftward phase boundary. Coins use gold flecks, a short trail and gentle spin; fuel uses turquoise flecks and a light wobble. After contact, pickup opacity eases from 100% to 72% over the first 12% of its duration and stays there, including docking. Size, spin and rotation share the same smooth travel progress. The last 10% of the flight stays visible at the exact icon center, at its measured icon size, before the credit appears and the icon pulses. Pickups never fade out behind a card. Arrival forces an immediate HUD snapshot rather than waiting for the periodic update. The new top-left fuel percentage and existing bottom fuel dial share the same displayed value.

HUD targets are measured from the real icon bounds and updated on resize, including safe-area positioning and icon size. The overlay uses the same CSS-pixel coordinate space with device-pixel-ratio capped at 2. Camera motion does not drag a flying pickup away from its screen path. The effect is bounded to 32 flights; excess rewards display immediately. Reduced-motion skips travel and pulse animation.

Pausing freezes pending deliveries; resume continues them. Restart clears all pending flights and credits. Crash/finish settle outstanding display credits before showing the result overlay. No currency, fuel, track or bike tuning changed.

Coin sound is a short layered metallic ping with a bounded rising streak; fuel has a distinct lower bubbling/rising refill cue. Web Audio synthesis works offline without extra downloads. Both respect saved sound settings, bounded polyphony, lifecycle pause and restart cleanup. No claim of physical-device loudness tuning; review the balance on the target phone.

Validation: contact event uniqueness, fuel-cap amount, independent delivery timing, pause with zero dt, no duplicate arrival, reset, bounded queue, exact flight endpoints, continuous direction/speed through the former lift boundary, zero-speed docking, bounded post-contact translucency, visible docking before delivery, distinct bounded audio cues, existing complete-course physics regression. Browser QA verified visible coin flights, count updates, fuel refill, pause/resume, restart, mute toggle and the lowered splash logo. Follow-up browser QA verified a flying coin overlapping the top-left card on top of its artwork, an unobstructed gas pedal, successful counter updates and a hidden effect canvas while paused.
